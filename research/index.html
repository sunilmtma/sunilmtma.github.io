Research

I am interested in working with robots on industrial use cases, and in simulating them in a digital twin environment before they run on the real system. My research interests are computer vision, robotics, digital twins and automation.

My PhD thesis, 6D Camera Pose Refinement and 3D Reconstruction via a Radiance Field-Based Approach: an industrial perspective, is supervised by Yohan Dupuis, Vincent Havard and Nicolas Ragot.

I built and released the first paired physical/digital-twin industrial RGB-D dataset, recorded with a stereo camera and a 16-camera OptiTrack motion-capture rig for millimetre-accurate ground truth and replayed along the identical trajectory in a Unity digital twin. On it I benchmark Structure-from-Motion back-ends and Gaussian Splatting variants across both domains, and I develop hybrid pipelines that replace part of the real data with synthetic views.

Projects

FUSION

Framework for Universal Software Integration in OpeN robotics

France 2030 “i-Démo régionalisé” project with the Normandy region and industrial partners Assystem, Orano and Conscience Robotics. It integrates AI and extended reality into robotics; my part is 3D reconstruction and 6D camera pose estimation in industrial environments. It funds my PhD.

REMOTED

Robotic EnvironMent hybridizatiOn rendering based on data-efficient radiance field TEchnologies and Digital twin

French–Hungarian PHC Balaton project (52934RC, Campus France) with ELTE University, Budapest, on data-efficient hybrid 3D reconstruction for industrial sites where real data is scarce. I am CESI's collaborator on it and have made two research visits to Budapest.
