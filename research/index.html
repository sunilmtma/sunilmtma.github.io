Research

Since March 2024 I have been a PhD candidate in Computer Science at CESI LINEACT (UR 7527) and ENSAM ParisTech (doctoral school ED SMI 432). My thesis, 6D Camera Pose Refinement and 3D Reconstruction via a Radiance Field-Based Approach: an industrial perspective, is directed by Yohan Dupuis, co-directed by Vincent Havard and co-supervised by Nicolas Ragot.

PhD work

Within the FUSION project, I work on 3D reconstruction of industrial environments from paired physical and digital twins: the same scene and the same camera path, once recorded in the real world and once rendered in simulation.

  • A paired physical/digital-twin dataset. I built and released the first paired physical/digital-twin industrial RGB-D dataset. It was recorded with a stereo camera and a 16-camera OptiTrack motion-capture rig for millimetre-accurate ground truth, then replayed along the identical trajectory in a Unity digital twin. [Dataset]
  • Benchmarking across the domain gap. On this dataset I benchmark Structure-from-Motion back-ends and 3D Gaussian Splatting variants on both the real and the rendered data, to measure how far results obtained in simulation carry over to the real world (ICPR 2026 workshop; journal paper under review).
  • 6D camera pose refinement. I evaluate how robust 3D Gaussian Splatting-based camera pose refinement stays when the input images are degraded, on lightly textured industrial objects (IECON 2025).
  • Hybrid reconstruction. I develop pipelines that replace part of the real images with synthetic views from the digital twin, to reduce how much real data has to be captured on site (in preparation).
  • Review. A systematic review of camera and object 6D pose estimation by radiance-field inversion, and of the evidence for its industrial readiness (in preparation).

Projects

FUSION · 2024 – 2028

Framework for Universal Software Integration in OpeN robotics

France 2030 “i-Démo régionalisé” project funded with the Normandy region, bringing together Conscience Robotics, Assystem, Orano and CESI. FUSION aims to make robotics easier to use by combining AI, extended reality and digital twins, so that robot missions can be designed and checked before they run. Its use case is robotised intervention in nuclear environments, which limits human exposure to radiation. FUSION funds my PhD.

REMOTED · 2025 – 2026

Robotic EnvironMent hybridizatiOn rendering based on data-efficient radiance field TEchnologies and Digital twin

French–Hungarian PHC Balaton project (52934RC, Campus France) between CESI LINEACT and ELTE University, Budapest. It works on data-efficient hybrid 3D reconstruction for industrial sites where real data is scarce, mixing real captures with views rendered from a digital twin. I am CESI's collaborator on the project. We received the REMOTED research travel grant in summer 2024, and I visited ELTE for two weeks in October 2025 and for one week in June 2026.
