Research

My PhD thesis, 6D Camera Pose Refinement and 3D Reconstruction via a Radiance Field-Based Approach: an industrial perspective, is supervised by Yohan Dupuis, Vincent Havard and Nicolas Ragot. I built and released the first paired physical/digital-twin industrial RGB-D dataset, recorded with a stereo camera and a 16-camera OptiTrack motion-capture rig for millimetre-accurate ground truth and replayed along the identical trajectory in a Unity digital twin. On it I benchmark Structure-from-Motion back-ends and Gaussian Splatting variants across both domains, and I develop hybrid pipelines that replace part of the real data with synthetic views.

FUSION

Framework for Universal Software Integration in OpeN robotics

France 2030 “i-Démo régionalisé” project with the Normandy region and industrial partners Assystem, Orano and Conscience Robotics. It integrates AI and extended reality into robotics; my part is 3D reconstruction and 6D camera pose estimation in industrial environments. It funds my PhD.

REMOTED

Robotic EnvironMent hybridizatiOn rendering based on data-efficient radiance field TEchnologies and Digital twin

French–Hungarian PHC Balaton project (52934RC, Campus France) with ELTE University, Budapest, on data-efficient hybrid 3D reconstruction for industrial sites where real data is scarce. I am CESI's collaborator on it and have made two research visits to Budapest.

Background

Since March 2024 I have been a doctoral researcher at the CESI LINEACT laboratory in Rouen, enrolled at ENSAM ParisTech (doctoral school ED SMI 432), working on computer vision, AI and robotics.

Before that, I did my master's thesis internship at ISIR, Sorbonne University, with Sinan Haliyo, Ferhat Sadak and Edison Gerena. I estimated the 3D orientation of optical microrobots from the microscope image stream, with applications to in vitro fertilisation. The work was published in Robotica and fed into the early stage of MovaLife, a microrobotics start-up.

My training started in mathematics. I hold a Master in Artificial Intelligence (M2 Santé-AI) from Université Bourgogne Europe, a Master in Mathematical Physics (M2 Math4Phys) from Université Marie & Louis Pasteur in Dijon, and a four-year Bachelor of Science in Mathematics from IISER Pune, India.

Teaching and mentoring

In 2026 I taught two courses at CESI School of Engineering in Rouen: Deep Learning and AI Ethics (45 hours, second-year master's students) and Machine Learning and AI (15 hours, fourth-year engineering students). The workshops covered neural network architectures, regression, classification and hyperparameter tuning on medical data, along with AI ethics and research integrity.

I also supervised two student projects on Gaussian Splatting:

  • Hybrid Reconstruction using Real and Synthetic Images of Industrial Objects via 3D Gaussian Splatting, a master's thesis internship (April to August 2026). It showed how far CAD-derived synthetic views can replace real acquisitions.
  • Benchmarking 3D Gaussian Splatting Techniques in Industry and Its Real Use Cases, a master's group project with three students (January to April 2026).
