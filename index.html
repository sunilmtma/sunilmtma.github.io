Sunil Choudhary

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at CESI LINEACT and ENSAM ParisTech in Rouen, France.

I want to put robots to work on real industrial tasks, and to build the digital twins where those robots are simulated and tested first. My PhD work is on 6D camera pose refinement and 3D reconstruction with radiance fields in industrial environments.

Research Interests:
Computer Vision, Robotics, Digital Twins, Automation.
