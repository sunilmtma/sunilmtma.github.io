Research

My PhD thesis, 6D Camera Pose Refinement and 3D Reconstruction via a Radiance Field-Based Approach: an industrial perspective, is supervised by Yohan Dupuis, Vincent Havard and Nicolas Ragot. I built and released the first paired physical/digital-twin industrial RGB-D dataset, recorded with a stereo camera and a 16-camera OptiTrack motion-capture rig for millimetre-accurate ground truth and replayed along the identical trajectory in a Unity digital twin. On it I benchmark Structure-from-Motion back-ends and Gaussian Splatting variants across both domains, and I develop hybrid pipelines that replace part of the real data with synthetic views.

Experience

  1. Mar 2024 – Mar 2027

    Doctoral Researcher, Computer Vision, AI and Robotics

    CESI LINEACT Laboratory (UR 7527), Rouen, France

    • Built and released the first paired physical/digital-twin industrial RGB-D dataset, with OptiTrack motion-capture ground truth replayed in a Unity twin.
    • Benchmarked Structure-from-Motion back-ends against Gaussian Splatting variants on pose accuracy, rendering fidelity and runtime; quantified the sim-to-real gap and showed that method rankings transfer between domains.
    • Developed a hybridization technique that narrows the gap by adding synthetic data and reducing the real data required.
    • Met project partners regularly and demonstrated results to funders.
